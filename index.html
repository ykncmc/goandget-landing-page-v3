04. QR-Powered Shop Connection

Turn every walk-in into a digital connection.

Your customers already visit your shop. Give them another reason to stay connected. Every Go&Get shop gets a unique QR code that can be displayed inside the store, at the billing counter or on promotional materials.

When customers scan the QR code, they can:

  • ✓ View your live offers.
  • ✓ Explore your shop profile.
  • ✓ Discover interesting products.
  • ✓ Save your shop for future reference.
  • ✓ Access the Go&Get app to explore more.

Businesses can also view the number of customers who have saved their shop through this QR experience.

One QR code. More ways to stay connected.
